As per Clause 12(e) of SEBI Circular dated December 03, 2009, Stock Brokers are required to settle running accounts at least once in every calendar quarter or calendar month, depending on the client’s preference. Clauses 8.1.1 and 8.1.4 of the Annexure to SEBI Circular dated September 26, 2016, mandated that the maximum period between two running account settlements shall not exceed 90 (ninety) days or 30 (thirty) days, respectively, as per the client’s preference. Clause 5.1 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, further states that the quarterly or monthly settlement, as the case may be, shall be carried out after considering the end-of-day (EOD) obligation of funds as on the date of settlement across all Exchanges. Clause 4.1 of SEBI Circular dated July 27, 2022, has now partially modified the above requirements by mandating that the running accounts shall be settled on the first Friday of each quarter for all clients after considering the EOD obligation across all Exchanges on the date of settlement. 45.
