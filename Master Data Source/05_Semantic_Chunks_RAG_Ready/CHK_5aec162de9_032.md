To summarize the findings made at paragraphs 47 – 60 above, I find that the Noticee has failed to settle the client accounts on 560 instances pertaining to 526 client accounts in violation of Clause 12(e) of SEBI Circular dated December 03, 2009, Clause 8.1.1 of Annexure to SEBI Circular dated September 26, 2016, C lause 5.1 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 and Clause 4.1 of SE BI Circular dated July 27, 2022. III. FAILURE TO SEND RETENTION STATEMENTS /SMS TO THE CLIENTS AT THE TIME OF SETTLEMENT AND NON-SUBMISSION OF DOCUMENTS TO THE INSPECTING AUTHORITY 62. The SCN alleged that the Noticee failed to produce the dispatch proof of retention statements in 457 instance s where the email sent to clients had bounced and did not provide the logs of SMS sent to clients at the time of settlement despite repeated requests. In view of the same , it has been alleged that the Noticee has violated Regulation 21 of Stock Brokers Regulation, SEBI Circular dated September 26, 2016 and Annexure A to SEBI Circular dated December 2009. 63. As per the provisions of Regulation 21(1), (2) and (3) of the Stock Brokers Regulations, 1992, the stockbrokers are obligated to produce documents / records / information in their custody or control to the inspecting authority, and allow them reasonable access to their premises.
