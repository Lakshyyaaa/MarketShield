With respect to 77 instances, the SCN alleged that the Noticee had faile d to settle the client accounts due to incorrect bank IFSC and failed to report the said account as untraceable to the exchange. The Noticee had contended before the DA that the accounts were duly settled but the amount s were returned due to incorrect Bank IFSC. The DA rejected the said submission of the Noticee and concluded that the client accounts cannot be considered as settled because the said client accounts were not reported as untraceable to the Stock Exchange as per applicable SEBI and Exchange Circulars. 21. As per clause 5.3 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, client’s running account shall be considered settled only by making actual payment into client’s bank account. Clause 5.4 of the said circular also mandates that a Stock Broker shall settle the client accounts within the next 3 working days, if a client does no transaction for more than 30 days. Further, as per BSE Notices dated February 10, 2020 and March 31, 2022, if a Stock Broker fails to settle any client account due to reasons such as incorrect Bank IFSC, they are required to set-aside the client funds in a separate bank account and report such client accounts as untraceable to the Stock Exchange. [page 16] Order in respect of Raghunandan Capital Private Ltd. Page 16 of 37 22. The Noticee has not refuted the allegation made in the SCN but has restated the submissions made before the DA that the accounts were settled but the amounts were returned due to incorrect Bank IFSC. I note that on 77 instances pertaining to 16 client accounts, the Noticee had failed to settle the running account of client within the prescribed 33 days (30+3) from the date of last transaction . In terms of clause 5.3 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 such accounts cannot be considered as settled because the actual payment was not made into the client accounts.
