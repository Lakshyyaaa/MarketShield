With respect to 172 instances pertaining to 169 client accounts, the SCN alleged that the Noticee did not calculate margin obligations across all exchanges while settling the client accounts . The Noticee has produced before me the financial ledgers in respect of the said accounts and submitted that such clients had open position in their trading account s across different exchanges for which margin was utilized and therefore such accounts were not required to be settled. 51. Clause 5.1 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 mandates that while settling the running accounts; the Stock Brokers have to consider EOD obligations across all the Exchanges on the date of settlement. The EOD obligations of the said 169 client accounts provided by the Clearing Corporation , when compared with the financial [page 27] Order in respect of Raghunandan Capital Private Ltd. Page 27 of 37 statement of client accounts submitted by the Noticee, reveals that the Noticee had not considered EOD obligations across all the exchanges while settling the said accounts, which resulted in retention of excess funds of the client . Therefore, I find that the Noticee failed to settle th e client accounts as per applicable provisions .
