On 1 instance pertaining to client account with code I11929 (for the period of March 2023), the Noticee has submitted that the account was duly settled. From the financial statements, it is noted that the Noticee settled the said account on April 24, 2023 even though the client had not carried out any transaction in the month of February- March 2023 i.e. for more than 30 days . As per Clause 5.4 of the SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, a running account should be settled within next 3 working days, if no transaction is carried out for more than 30 days . In the present case, it is evident that more than 33 days had elapsed between the date of transaction and settlement . Therefore, I find that the No ticee failed to settle the account as per SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021. 31. Further, on 1 instance pertaining to client account with code DBS1326 (for the period September 2023), the Noticee submitted that the account was duly settled. However, from the financial statement of the client account , I note that the account was not settled and the amount had returned due to incorrect Bank IFSC. The Noticee despite failing to settle the account due to incorrect Bank IFSC had not reported the account as untraceable in the exchange reporting as per BSE Notices dated February 10, 2020 and March 31, 2022. Therefore, I find that the said account was not settled as per SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021. 32. In view of the above, I find that the Noticee had failed to settle t he running client accounts on 28 instances pertaining to 28 client accounts within the prescribed timelines and violated SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021.
