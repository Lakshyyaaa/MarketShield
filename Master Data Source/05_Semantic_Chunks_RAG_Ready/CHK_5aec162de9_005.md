Submissions pertaining to retention of client funds in excess of permissible obligations. 11.3. The Noticee provided instance wise response for the alleged 560 instances to submit the following: i. On 105 instances, the client accounts were settled before the due date (i.e. before the first Friday of the quarter) and cannot be considered as a violation of clause 5.4 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021. ii. On 281 instances, the client accounts were already settled on the due date. iii. On 172 instances, the clients had open position in their trading account for which margin was utilized and therefore such accounts were not required to be settled. iv. On 2 instances, fresh funds were received on the date of settlement, therefore such accounts did not require settlement. [page 6] Order in respect of Raghunandan Capital Private Ltd. Page 6 of 37 11.4. The Noticee has stated that SEBI Circular dated July 27, 2022 was a recent update at that point in time. Therefore, its back-office system was not updated and the settlement was done as per the earlier circular, which had prescribed 90 days for settlement of accounts. 11.5. The Noticee has submitted that the objective of the SEBI Circular dated Ju ly 27, 2022 was to prevent the Stock Broker from retaining or utilizing the funds of the client on or after the due date. Theref ore, the Noticee has argued that in 105 instances where the funds were settled before the due date because of operational compulsion, the same shall not be deemed a violation but a mere technical error. 11.6. The Noticee has provided a sample wise response of 15 instances along with supporting retention statement to explain its calculation of margin obligation and has also submitted that presently settlement is being done as per SEBI Circular dated July 27, 2022 and all the client accounts mentioned in the i nspection report have been settled and there are no complaints from its clients reg arding non-settlement of funds.
