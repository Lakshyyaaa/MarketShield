A portfolio based margining approach shall be adopted to take an integrated view of the risk involved in the portfolio of each individual client comprising his positions in futures contracts across different maturities. The client -wise margins would be grossed across various clients at the Trading/Clearing Member level. The proprietary positions of the Trading/Clearing Member would be treated as that of a client. 5.2.4 Real time computation The computation of worst scenario loss would have two components. The fir st is the valuation of the portfolio under the various scenarios of price changes. At the second stage, these scenario contract values would be applied to the actual portfolio positions to compute the portfolio values and the initial margin. The exchanges shall update the scenario contract values at least 5 times in the day, which may be carried out by taking the closing price of the previous day at the start of trading and the prices at 11:00 a.m., 12:30 p.m., 2:00 p.m. and at the end of the trading sessio n. The latest available scenario contract values would be applied to member/client portfolios on a real time basis. 5.2.5 Calendar spread margins A currency futures position at one maturity which is hedged by an offsetting position at a different maturity would be treated as a calendar spread.
