The CC shall pay-out funds to the selling CM on T+2 day Further,  In case of a settlement shortage of Rs . 5 lakh or more, the trading facility of all trading members clearing through the buying CM shall be withdrawn in the Currency Derivatives Segment and the securities pay -out to the buying CM shall be withheld.  If the buying CM is short for an amount of Rs . 2 lakh or more on six or more occasions in the preceding three months, the trading facility of all the trading members clearing through the buying CM shall be withdrawn in the Currency Derivatives Segment and the securities pay -out to the buying CM shall be withheld.  A penalty of 0.07% per day shall be levied on the amount of the shortage. The trading facility shall be restored and securities withheld shall be released on the buying CM making good the shortage amount in all the above cases.
