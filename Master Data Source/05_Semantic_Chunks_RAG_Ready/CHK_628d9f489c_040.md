In-principle approval: 2. An issuer desirous of issuing and listing non-convertible securities or municipal debt securities, shall make an application for in -principle approval to the stock Exchange(s), in terms of Regulation 6 of the NCS Regulations or Regulation 4A of the ILDM Regulations, respectively, complete in all respects, including the submissions and disclosures, as may be specified by the stock exchange(s).
