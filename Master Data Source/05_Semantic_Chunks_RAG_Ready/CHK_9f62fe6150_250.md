The final report on said VAPT should be submitted to SEBI after approval from Standing Committee on Technology (SCOT) of respective MIIs, within 1 month of completion of VAPT activity. 41) Any gaps/vulnerabilities detected have to be remedied on immediate basis and compliance of closure of findings identified during VAPT shall be submitted to SEBI within 3 months post the submission of final VAPT report to SEBI. 42) In addition, MIIs should also perform vulnerability scanning and conduct penetration testing prior to the commissioning of a new system which is a critical system or part of an existing critical system.
