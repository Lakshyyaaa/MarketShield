The valuation or the marking to market of such units shall be carried out on a daily basis. The valuation / marking to market of all securities, including debt securities, dated government securities and T-bills, shall be carried out daily, with appropriate haircuts. Debt securities shall be a cceptable only if they are investment grade. Haircuts shall be at least 10% with daily mark to market. The total exposure of the clearing corporation to the debt or equity securities of any company shall not exceed 75% of the trade guarantee fund or 15% of the total liquid assets of the clearing corporation / house whichever is lower.
