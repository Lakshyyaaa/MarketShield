Exchanges shall put in place a mechanism to ensure that the LES does not create artificial volumes, does not take away liquidity form the market, is not manipulative in nature and shall not lead to misselling of the product in the market. ii. The exchange shall have systems and defined procedures in place to monitor collusion between brokers indulging in trades solely for seeking incentives and prevent payment of incentives in such cases. iii. Incentives shall not be provided for the trades where the counterparty is self, i.e., same UCC is on both sides of the transaction. iv. Any violations of clauses in this para shall be viewed most seriously. 1.9.6.
