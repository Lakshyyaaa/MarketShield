Ministry of Finance vide notification number S.O.1002(E) dated March 01, 2024 has notified list of goods under clause (bc ) of Section 2 of SCRA, 1956, thereby superseding the earlier notification number S.O. 3068(E) dated September 27, 2016 issued on the same subject. The new list of goods notified vide notification dated March 01, 2024, includes additional thirteen ( 13) goods and alloys for five (5) metals, thereby increasing the list of goods on which derivatives can be launched from ninety-one (91) to one hundred and four (104).
