The Vault Manager shall take an acknowledgment receipt from the BO/authorized representative and simultaneously extinguish the EGR corresponding to the gold. 4.1.9. The Vault Manager shall intimate the extinguishment of EGR to the Depository to carry out extinguishment of such EGR from the demat account of the Beneficial Owner. 4.2. Cut-off time 4.2.1. The Vault Manager shall allow withdrawal of gold from 10:00 AM to 03:00 PM. 4.2.2. The requests for withdrawal of gold made into the recognized vault during the prescribed time shall be processed by the Vault Manager on the same working day. ****
