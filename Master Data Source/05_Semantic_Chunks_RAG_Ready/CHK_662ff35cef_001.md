# SEBI | Standard Operating Procedure for handling of Stock Exchange outage and extension of trading hours thereof in Commodity Derivatives segment Source PDF: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/may-2024/1716808675206.pdf Landing page: https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/may-2024/standard-operating-procedure-for-handling-of-stock-exchange-outage-and-extension-of-trading-hours-thereof-in-commodity-derivatives-segment_83588.html [page 1] Page 1 of 5 CIRCULAR SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2024/62 May 27, 2024 The Managing Directors / Chief Executive Officers All Recognised Stock Exchanges having Commodity Derivatives Segment Dear Sir / Madam, Subject: Standard Operating Procedure for handling of Stock Exchange outage and extension of trading hours thereof in Commodity Derivatives segment 1. SEBI vide circular dated January 09, 2023 has prescribed standard operating procedure for handling stock exchange outage and extension of trading hours thereof in Cash Market and Equity Derivatives segment. 2. In continuation of the same, s tandard operating procedure for handling stock exchange outage and extension of trading hours thereof in Commodity Derivatives segment was deliberated by Commodity Derivatives Advisory Committee (CDAC) of SEBI. Accordingly, the framework for Commodity Derivatives segment has been prepared and placed as Annexure to the Circular. 3. The stock exchanges should ensure necessary changes in the systems to extending market hours as specified in this circular. Accordingly, the circular shall be effective from July 01, 2024. 4. The Stock Exchanges are advised to bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of their members and also to disseminate the same on their website. 5. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11 (1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities market. 6. The Circular is issued with the approval of the competent authority. [page 2] Page 2 of 5 7. This circular is available on SEBI website www.sebi.gov.in under the category “Circulars” and “Info for Commodity Derivatives”.
