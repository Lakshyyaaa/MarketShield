In this regard, the following is specified – 4.2.1. Schemes of AIFs, whose Liquidation Period has expired or shall expire on or before July 24, 2024 (i.e. within three months from the date of notification of AIF Regulations Amendment), shall be granted a fresh Liquidation Period till April 24, 2025. 4.2.2. The aforesaid fresh Liquidation Period shall be av ailable only to such schemes that do not have any pending investor complaint with respect to non -receipt of funds / securities as on April 25, 2024, i.e., the date of notification of AIF Regulations Amendment. In case a scheme has pending investor complaint(s) with respect to non -receipt of funds / securities, such scheme may avail the fresh Liquidation Period upon resolution of investor complaint(s). However, the fresh Liquidation Period shall be available only from the date of resolution of the complaint till April 24, 2025. 4.2.3. During the fresh Liquidation Period, the scheme shall fully liquidate its investments, or distribute the investments in -specie or opt for the Dissolution Period. 5.
