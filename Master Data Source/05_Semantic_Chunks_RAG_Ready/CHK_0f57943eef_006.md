(1) The appointment, renewal of appointment and termination of service of the managing director of a depository shall be subject to prior approval of the Board. (2) Every depository shall, subject to the guidelines issued by the Board from time to time, determine the qualification, manner of appointment, terms and conditions of appointment and other procedural formalities relating to the selection/ appointment of the managing director. (3) The appointment of the managing director shall be for a tenure not less than three years and not exceeding five years.
