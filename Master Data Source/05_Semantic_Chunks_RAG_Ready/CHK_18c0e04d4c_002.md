Form ISR-5: Simplified Procedure for Transmission of Securities (Death of Holder) * **Purpose:** Legal transmission of physical/demat securities upon the death of sole or joint holders. * **Simplified Documentation Thresholds (SEBI Fourth LODR Amendment):** - **Up to ₹5,00,000 (Physical) / ₹15,00,000 (Demat) per issuer company:** Simplified documentation route (No Probate or Succession Certificate required). - **Above ₹5,00,000 (Physical) / ₹15,00,000 (Demat):** Requires Succession Certificate / Letter of Administration / Probated Will. * **Mandatory Documents Required (Nominee Registered):** 1. Form ISR-5 duly filled and signed by nominee. 2. Original / Notarized Death Certificate of deceased holder. 3. Self-attested PAN and Aadhaar of Nominee. 4. Client Master List (CML) of Nominee's Demat Account. * **Mandatory Documents Required (No Nominee - Legal Heirs):** 1. Form ISR-5 signed by all legal heirs. 2. Notarized Indemnity Bond from all legal heirs. 3. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from non-claiming legal heirs (or Deed of Relinquishment). 4. Legal Heirship Certificate / Family Tree certified by Revenue Authority / Tahsildar (for threshold limits). --- ## 6. Form SH-13 / Form ISR-13: Nomination Form for Registering a Nominee * **Purpose:** Registration of a nominee for physical share folios under Section 72 of Companies Act 2013 and SEBI LODR. * **Requirements:** 1. Duly filled Form SH-13 with Folio number, Company Name, and Nominee details (Name, DOB if minor, Address, Relationship, PAN). 2. Specimen signature of Nominee. 3. Signatures of all joint holders and one witness. --- ## 7.
