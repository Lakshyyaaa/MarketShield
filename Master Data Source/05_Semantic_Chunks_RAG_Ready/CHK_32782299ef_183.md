VI. DR Drills / Testing a) DR drills should be conducted on quarterly basis. In case of exchanges, these drills should be closer to real life scenario (trading days) with minimal notice to DR staff involved. b) During the drills, the staff based at PDC should not be involved in supporting operations in any manner.
