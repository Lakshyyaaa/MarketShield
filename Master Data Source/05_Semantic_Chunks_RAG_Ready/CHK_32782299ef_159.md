Revision in professional fee of arbitra tors: The arbitrator fee shall be upwardly revised to Rs.18,000/ - (Rs. Eighteen thousand) per case. Consequent to this upward revision, the additional expenses attributable to a client over and above the fee structure as specified above in Arbitration Fees, shall be borne by the client (wherever applicable) and Exchange equally. The total expense attributable to the member has to be borne by the concerned member. 14.3.18.
