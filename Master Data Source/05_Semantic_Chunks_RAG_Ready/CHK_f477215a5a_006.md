Price Bands For every IRF contract, Stock Exchanges shall set an initial price band at 3% of the previous closing price thus preventing acceptance of orders for execution that are placed beyond the set band. Whenever a trade in any contract is executed at the highest/lowest price of the band, stock exchanges may expand the price band for that contract by 0.5% in that direction after 30 minutes after taking into accou nt market trend. However, no t more than 2 expansions in the price band shall be allowed within a day. Further, SEBI in consultation with RBI may halt the trading in case of extreme volatility in the IRF market. 15. Risk Management Framework Clearing Corporations shall determine appropriate risk management framework for the product and submit the same to SEBI for approval. The Initial Margin requirement shall be based on a worst case loss of a portfolio of an individual client across various scenarios of price changes. The various scenarios of price changes would be so computed so as to cover a 99% VaR over a one day horizon. Further Extreme Loss margins and calendar spread margins shall also be prescribed by clearing corporations. Margins shall be deducted from the liquid assets of the clearing member on an on line, real time basis.
