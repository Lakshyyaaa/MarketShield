Know Your Depositor 12.7.1. The Clearing Corporation shall ensure that the WSP complies with Know Your Depositor (KYD) Policy as prescribed by the Clearing Corporation from time to time. 12.7.2. The Clearing Corporation shall ensure that they and the WSP shall, at any point of time be able to identify the depositor of the goods deposited in registered warehouses, and the actual beneficiary (in case the depositor and the beneficiary are different) of the deposited/stored commodities. 12.8.
