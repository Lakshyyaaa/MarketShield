For clause 1, the following clause shall be substituted, namely:- “1 The Issuer agrees that in addition to the covenants in this part of this agreement executed between the Issuer and the Exchange, the issuer shall be bound by the covenants provided in the equity Listing Agreement Provided that covenants in the Equity Listing Agreement, which are not applicable to issue of debt securities in terms of the SEBI (Issue of Listing of Debt Securities) Regulations 2008, shall not be applicable in respect of this Listing Agreement. Provided further that the issuer who has submitted any information to the Exchange in compliance with the disclosure requirements under the equity Listing Agreement, need not re-submit any such information under this Listing Agreement without prejudice to any power conferred on the Exchange or SEBI or any other authority under any law to seek any such information from the issuer.” 2.
