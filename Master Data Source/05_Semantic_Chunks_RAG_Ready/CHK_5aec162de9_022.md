As per clause 8.1.3 of SEBI Circular dated September 26, 2016, settlement of running client account shall be made through electronic fund transfer. Further, clause 5.5 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 states that upon failure of electronic payment instructions, settlement can be made through physical modes like cheque and the date of realization of such cheque shall be considered as the settlement date. From the statement of accoun t provided by the Noticee, I note that the Noticee has not explained why cheque was issued without first attempting an electronic fund transfer. The Noticee has also not explained why the account was not reported as untraceable to the Stock Exchange when such account was not settled for around 9 months. Further, since the cheque issued by the Noticee was never realized, the date of settlement of account has to be considered as 06-01-2023 i.e. the day on which actual payment was done.
