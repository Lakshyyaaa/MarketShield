Handling exigencies : The entity undertak es to establish appropriate safeguards and procedures to deal with exigencies like suspension or cessation of trading in products or services or securities as specified in clause 5.2 of this Chapter, cancellation of orders or transactions by the investors and sellers, malfunctions or erroneous use of its systems by investors and sellers, or other unforeseen situations. 9. Data integrity - Preservation, access and use of data: The entity undertakes to: 10.1. maintain all data relating to its activities in an easily retrievable media.
