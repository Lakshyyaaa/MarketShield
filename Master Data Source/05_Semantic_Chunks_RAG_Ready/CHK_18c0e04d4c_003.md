Signatures of all joint holders and one witness. --- ## 8. Form IEPF-5 Instruction Sheet: e-Form Filing for Unclaimed Shares & Dividends * **Purpose:** Detailed step-by-step procedure for reclaiming shares or dividends transferred to the IEPF Authority after 7 consecutive years of non-claim. * **Filing Requirements:** 1. File online e-Form IEPF-5 on MCA Portal (mca.gov.in) with Claimant PAN, Bank Account, CIN, Folio/DP-Client ID, Amount, Share Quantity. 2. Submit physical dossier to Company Nodal Officer within 15 days containing: Printed IEPF-5, SRN slip, Original Share Certificates, Cancelled Cheque, Rs 1 Revenue Stamp Advance Receipt, CML of Demat Account, Aadhaar and PAN. 3. Verification Report submitted by Company Nodal Officer to IEPF Authority within 30 days.
