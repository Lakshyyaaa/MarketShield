On 6 instances pertaining to 6 clients, it is noted that the clients had credited monies into their respective accounts but no transactions were carried out for more than 30 days since date of such credit . As per Clause 5.4 of the SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, the client accounts should be settled within the next 3 working days, if no transaction is carried out for more than 30 days (i.e. 33 days from the date of last transaction). In the said 6 instances, it is observed that although no transaction/trade was carried out by the clients for more than 30 days, the Noticee failed to settle the accounts within the next 3 working days and there was a delay in settlement .
