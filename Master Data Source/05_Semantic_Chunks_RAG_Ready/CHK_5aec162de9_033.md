SEBI Circular dated December 03, 2009 mandates the Stock Broker to send the statement of accounts containing inter-alia extract from client ledger for funds & securities along with a statement explaining the retention of funds/securities (“retention statement ”) to the clients during the settlement of accounts. Clause 8.1.4 states that the retention statement shall be sent within five days from the date when the account is considered to be settled. Further, clause 5.8 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 states t hat the Stock Broker shall send intimation of running account settlement to the clients by SMS on mobile number and by email. [page 34] Order in respect of Raghunandan Capital Private Ltd. Page 34 of 37 64. The Noticee had submitted before the DA that on 455 out of 457 instances, the retention statements were dispatched physically; and on 2 instances the retention statement was again sent after non-receipt of earlier digital and physical statement. The DA rejected the said submission of the Noticee by observing that the Noticee had provided only 11 copies of proof of delivery ( POD) receipts out of total 457 instances and it had not provided the dispatch register to substantiate its claim that retention statements were sent physically . Therefore, the DA concluded that th e Noticee had failed to send retention statements on 457 instances. 65. The Noticee has submitted before me a copy of the dispatch register w.r.t 457 instances along with 19 copies of POD receipts to substantiate its claims. The Noticee has further argued that the dispatch register was not submitted before the DA because the same was never sought from it. On perusal of the dispatch register submitted before me, it is observed that 11 POD receipts submitted before the DA do not find mention in the dispatch register. The Noticee has also not explained why the dispatch register was not produced before the inspecting authorities.
