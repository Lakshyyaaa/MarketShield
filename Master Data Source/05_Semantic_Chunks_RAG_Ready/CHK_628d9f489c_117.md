RESPONSIBILITIES OF INVESTORS (EXPECTATIONS FROM THE INVESTORS): 1. Investors should read offer documents, application form, and issue related literature carefully and fully before investing. 2. Investors should fully understand the terms of investment and timelines involved in the issue process as disclosed in the offer document, application form, and issue related literature. 3. Investor should consult his or her own tax consultant with respect to the specific tax implications. 4. Shareholders should ensure to register their correct email ID with the company or depository for timely updates on corporate actions, takeover, etc. 5. Investors should ensure active demat/ broking account before investing. [page 180] INVESTOR CHARTER- PRIVATE PLACEMENT OF NON-CONVERTIBLE SECURITIES VISION STATEMENT: To continuously earn trust of investors and emerge as a solution provider with integrity. MISSION STATEMENT: 1. Act in investors’ best interests by understanding needs and developing solutions. 2. Enhance and customise value generating capabilities and services. 3. Disseminate complete information to investors to enable informed investment decision. DESCRIPTION OF ACTIVITIES/ BUSINESS OF THE ENTITY: Act as Arranger to Private Placement, if appointed by the Issuer; DETAILS OF SERVICES PROVIDED TO INVESTORS: 1. Issuers disclosure of all covenants of the issue (including side letters, accelerated payment clause, etc.) in the placement memorandum. 2.
