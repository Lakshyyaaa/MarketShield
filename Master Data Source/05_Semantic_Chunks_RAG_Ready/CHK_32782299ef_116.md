Security Deposit (SD) i. The Clearing Corporation shall ensure that the accredited WSP has furnished a refundable security deposit wherein: a) Such security deposit shall be a minimum amount of INR50 lakhs. b) Such security deposit shall be in the form of Cash / Bank Fixed Deposits / Bank Guarantee. ii. Security deposit shall not be released until six months after cancellation or surrender of the accreditation of the WSP or until after satisfaction of every claim against the deposit, whichever is later. 12.4.4.
