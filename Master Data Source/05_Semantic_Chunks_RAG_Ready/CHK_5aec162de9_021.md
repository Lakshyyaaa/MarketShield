The Noticee failed to settle the said client accounts within th e maximum permissible timeline of 33 days as per the SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021. 37. On 6 instances pertaining to 6 clients, it is noted that money was credited to respective client accounts and no transaction was carried out for more than 30 days. As per clause 5.4 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, c lient accounts in which there are no transactions for more than 30 days, have to be settled within the next three working days. The Noticee failed to settle the said client accounts within the maximum permissible time i.e. 33 days and there was delay in settlement in violation of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 .
