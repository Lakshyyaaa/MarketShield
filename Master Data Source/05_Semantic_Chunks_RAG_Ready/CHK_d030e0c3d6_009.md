In case of material changes signifi cantly influencing the decision of the investor to continue to be invested in the AIF, the process as mentioned hereunder shall be complied with. Such changes shall include, but not be limited to the following: (a) Change in sponsor/manager (not including an i nternal restructuring within the group), (b) Change in control of sponsor/manager, (c) Change in fee structure or hurdle rate which may result in higher fees being charged to the unit holders. The following process shall be followed by the AIF: (i) Existing unit holders who do not wish to continue post the change shall be provided an exit option.
