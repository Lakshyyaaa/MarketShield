The Stock Exchanges interested to introduce physical settlement should: a. put in place proper systems and procedures for smooth implementation of physical settlement. b. make necessary amendments to the relevant bye -laws, rules and regulations for implementation of physical settlement. c. bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of all categories of market participants, including the general public, and also to disseminate the d. same on their websites.
