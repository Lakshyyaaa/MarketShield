46 instances where running accounts of clients were not settled despite the accounts being due for settlement 33. With respect to 46 instances , the SCN has alleged that the Noticee had incorrectly stated the client accounts did not require settlement because more than 30 days had not elapsed from the date of last transaction. I note that the analysis of these 46 instances can be further sub -divided into the following 38, 2 and 6 instances based on difference in circumstances. 35. On 38 instances pertaining to 38 client accounts, I note that more than 30 days had elapsed between the date of last trade and the date of settlement. As per Clause 5.4 of the SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 a running account should be settled within next 3 working days, if no transaction is carried out for more than 30 days (i.e. 33 days from the date of last transact ion).
