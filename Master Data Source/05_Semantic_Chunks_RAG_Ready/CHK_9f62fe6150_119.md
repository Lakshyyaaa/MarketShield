Manner of filing/inviting claims from the Investors/Clients I. In accordance with its byelaws, rules or regulations, the stock exchange shall publish a notice inviting the legitimate claimants to file claims against the defaulter member within a specified period of time called as the ‘specified period’. II. The specified period shall be a minimum period of 90 days. III.
