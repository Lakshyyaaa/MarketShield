In consultation with the exchanges, it has been decided to streamline the arbitration mechanism available at exchanges for arbitration of disputes (claims, complaints, differences, etc.) arisi ng between a client and a member (Broker, Trading Member and Clearing Member) across various market segments. 14.3.3.
