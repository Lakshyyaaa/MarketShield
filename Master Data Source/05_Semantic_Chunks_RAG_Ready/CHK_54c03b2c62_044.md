Monitoring mechanism a) Stock Exchanges shall put in place necessary mechanism for monitoring and enforcing limits of FPIs in IRFs. b) Stock Exchanges shall aggregate net long position in IRF of all FPIs taken together at the end of the day and shall jointly publish/ disseminate the same on their website on daily basis. c) Once 90% of the limit is utilized, Stock Exchanges shall put in plac e necessary mechanism to get alerts and publish on their websites the available limit, on a daily basis. d) In case, there is any breach of the threshold limit, the FPI/s whose investment caused the breach shall square off their excess position/s within five trading days or by expiry of contract, whichever is earlier. 13.
