It is also emphasized that, while revising the transaction charges, the stock exchanges shall also comply with the following guidelines: i. Its system is capable of handling additional load. ii. It does not affect the existing risk management system. iii. It does not favour selective trades or selective category of investor. iv. It does not encourage generation of artificial demand. v.
