# SEBI Standardized Investor Service Request Forms (ISR-1 to ISR-5, ISR-13, SH-13, SH-14, IEPF-5) Official Source: SEBI Master Circular for RTAs `SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/91` and SEBI LODR Regulations. --- ## 1. Form ISR-1: Request for Registering PAN, KYC Details or Bank Details * **Purpose:** Mandatory update of PAN, Address, Email ID, Mobile Number, Demat Account details, and Bank Account details for physical share folios. * **Target Authority:** Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA) / Issuer Company. * **Mandatory Documents Required:** 1. Self-attested copy of PAN Card of all holders. 2. Proof of Address (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Driving License / Utility Bill < 3 months). 3. Client Master List (CML) of Demat Account issued by Depository Participant (DP). 4. Original Cancelled Cheque leaf with pre-printed name OR Bank Passbook/Statement attested by Bank. * **Turnaround Timeline:** 21 calendar days from date of receipt by RTA. --- ## 2. Form ISR-2: Confirmation of Signature of Securities Holder by Bank Manager * **Purpose:** Validation of signature when signature is missing, unrecorded, or mismatched in RTA records. * **Mandatory Requirements:** 1. Form ISR-2 filled and signed by the security holder. 2. Attestation by Bank Manager with Manager Name, Employee Code, Bank Seal, Branch Address, Contact Number, and Date. 3. Original Cancelled Cheque leaf or Bank Account Statement verified by the same bank branch. --- ## 3. Form ISR-3: Declaration of Opting-out of Nomination * **Purpose:** Explicit declaration by physical share single holder to opt out of registering a nominee. * **Mandatory Requirements:** 1. Form ISR-3 signed by the sole holder. 2. Witness signature (Name, Address, Signature of one witness). * **Note:** SEBI strongly recommends registering a nominee via Form SH-13 instead of opting out. --- ## 4. Form ISR-4: Request for Issue of Duplicate Certificate and Other Service Requests * **Purpose:** Processing requests for: - Issue of Duplicate Share Certificate (Lost/Stolen/Mutilated) - Claim from Unclaimed Suspense Account - Renewal / Exchange of Share Certificate - Endorsement / Sub-division / Splitting of Certificate - Consolidation of Folios / Certificates - Transmission / Transposition * **Mandatory Procedures for Duplicate Share Certificate:** 1.
