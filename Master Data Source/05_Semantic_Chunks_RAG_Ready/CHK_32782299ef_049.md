Eligibility and Jurisdiction i. Such EFEs shall have actual exposure to Indian physical commodity markets. ii. Such EFE is resident in a country/jurisdiction whose se curities market regulator and/or commodity derivatives market regulator is a signatory to IOSCO’s MMoU (Appendix A Signatories) or a signatory of a bilateral MoU with SEBI. Provided that such EFE is not resident in a country identified in the public statement of Financial Action Task Force as: a) a jurisdiction having a strategic Anti -Money Laundering or Combating the Financing of Terrorism deficiencies to which counter measures apply; or b) a jurisdiction that has not made sufficient progress in addressing the deficiencies or has not committed to an action plan developed with the Financial Action Task Force to address the deficiencies.
