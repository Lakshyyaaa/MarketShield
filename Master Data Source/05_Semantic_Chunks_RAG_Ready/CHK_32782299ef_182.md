Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery (DR)171 16.4.1. The stock exchanges with commodity derivatives segment shall have BCP & DR policy in place and implement the broad guidelines regarding the setting up of Disaster Recovery Site (DRS) and Near Site (NS), Configuration of DRS/NS with Primary Data Centre (PDC ), DR drills / Testing, BCP DR Policy as mentioned below: I. The exchanges should have in place Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Site (DRS) so as to maintain data and transaction integrity. II. Apart from DRS, exchanges should also have a Near Site (NS) to ensure zero data loss. III. The DRS should be set up sufficiently away, i.e. in a different seismic zone, from Primary Data Centre (PDC) to ensure that both DRS and PDC are not affected by the same disasters. IV.
