On 2 instances pertaining to the client account with code AHM472 (for the periods April-May 2025), the Noticee has contended that the said account was settled on due date. From the financial statements of the said client account, it is noted that the client had not carried out any trade in the months of April – May 2025. The Noticee had issued a cheque to settle the client account but the same was not cleared and reversed on June 14, 2022 due to lapse of more than 3 months. Clause 5.5. of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 stipulates that when physical payment instrument s (viz. cheque or dem and draft) are issued by the Stock Brokers towards the settlement of running account, the date of realization of physical instrument into client’s bank account shall be considered as settlement date and not the date of issue of physical instrument. In the present case , more than 33 days had lapsed without any transaction being carried out by the Client and the cheque issued by the Noticee was not realised within the prescribed time of 33 days. Further, the Noticee has not provided any explanation regarding the delay in settlement. Therefore, I find that that the Noticee has violated SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 in settlement of the said client account. 29.
