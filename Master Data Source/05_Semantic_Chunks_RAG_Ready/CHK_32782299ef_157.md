Speeding up grievance redressal mechanism: In order to have faster implementation of award and to discourage delayed filling of arbitrations by members, the additional fees payable by members who file their claim beyond the prescribed time -lines shall be non -refundable even if the arbitration award goes in favor of the member 153. The additional fees charged from the trading members, if the claim is filed beyond the prescribed timeline, if any, to be deposited in the IPF of the respective Stock Exchange.154 14.3.12.
