Margin Pr ovisions for Intra-day crystallized losses 85 : In order to mitigate the risk arising out of accumulation of crystallized obligations incurred on account of intra -day squaring off of positions, the stock exchanges shall adopt the following: i. The intra -day crystallized losses shall be monitored and blocked by Clearing Corporations from the free collateral on a real-time basis only for those transactions which are subject to upfront margining. For this purpose, crystallised losses can be offset against crystallised profits at a client level, if any. ii. If crystallised losses exceed t he free collateral available with the Clearing Corporation, risk reduction mode shall be followed. iii. Crystallised losses shall be calculated based on weighted average prices of trades executed. iv.
