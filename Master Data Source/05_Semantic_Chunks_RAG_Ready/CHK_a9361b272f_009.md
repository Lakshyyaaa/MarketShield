The Liquid Net Worth is defined as under: total liquid assets deposited with the exchange / clearing corporation / house towards initial margin and capital adequacy, LESS initial margin applicable to the total gross open positions at any gi ven point of time on all trades to be cleared through the clearing member. The clearing member‟s liquid net worth must satisfy both the conditions given below on a real time basis: Condition 1: Liquid Net Worth shall not be less than Rs 50 lacs at any point of time. Condition 2: The mark to market value of gross open positions at any point of time of all trades cleared through the cle aring member shall not exceed 33 1/3 (thirty three one by three) times his liquid networth. The notional value of gross open positions at any point in time in the case of Index Futures shall not exceed 33 1/3 (thirty three one by three) times the liquid net worth of a member. Exposure limits are in addition to the initial margin requirements.
