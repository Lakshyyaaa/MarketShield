Regulation 9 (b) and 9 (f) read with Schedule II (Code of Conduct) of the Stock Brokers Regulations, requires the Stock B rokers to ensure compliance with the provisions of the SEBI Act, Rules, Regulations, as well as the circulars and directives issued by SEBI and the stock exchanges. SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021, through its Clause 5.1, mandates that stock brokers must settle the running accounts of their clients at least once every 30 or 90 days, as per the client’s preference, after considering the end-of-day (EOD) fund obligations across all exchanges on the settlement date .

Clause 5.4 of the same circular further stipulates that if a client maintains a credit balance but has not executed any transactions for 30 calendar days following the last transaction, the Stock Broker is required to settle such client’s account within three working days, irrespective of the date when the running account was previously settled (i.e. 33 days from the last transaction) . Additionally, as clarified by BSE Notices dated February 10, 2020, and March 31, 2022, in situations where the broker is unable to settle a client’s account due to the non -availability of requisite bank details or similar reasons, the Stock Broker has to inter-alia open separate bank account, imme diately set aside the funds and submit the details of such accounts to the exchange on quarterly basis for regulatory oversight. 18. SEBI Circular dated December 03, 2009 mandates the Stock Broker to send the statement of accounts containing inter-alia extract from client ledger for funds & securities along with a statement explaining the retention of funds/securities (“retention statement ”) to the clients during the settlement of accounts. Clause 8.1.4 states that the retention statement shall be sent within five days from the date when the account is considered to be settled. Further, clause 5.8 of SEBI Circular dated June 16, 2021 states t hat the Stock Broker shall send intimation of running account settlement to the clients by SMS on mobile number and by email.
