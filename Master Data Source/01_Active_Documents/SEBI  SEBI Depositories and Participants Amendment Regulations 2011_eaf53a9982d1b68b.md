(4) The application for grant of a certificate of permanent registration shall be accompanied by details of the changes that have taken place in the information that was submitted to the Board while seeking initial registration or renewal, as the case may be, and a declaration statin g that no changes other than those as mentioned in such details have taken place. (7) On the grant of a certificate of permanent registration the participant shall be liable to pay registration fee as specified in the Second Schedule of these re gulations, within a period of fifteen days of receipt of intimation from the Board.
