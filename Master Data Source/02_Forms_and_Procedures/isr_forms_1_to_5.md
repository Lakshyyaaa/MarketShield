# SEBI Standardized Investor Service Request Forms (ISR-1 to ISR-5, ISR-13, SH-13, SH-14, IEPF-5)

Official Source: SEBI Master Circular for RTAs `SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/91` and SEBI LODR Regulations.

---

## 1. Form ISR-1: Request for Registering PAN, KYC Details or Bank Details
* **Purpose:** Mandatory update of PAN, Address, Email ID, Mobile Number, Demat Account details, and Bank Account details for physical share folios.
* **Target Authority:** Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA) / Issuer Company.
* **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  1. Self-attested copy of PAN Card of all holders.
  2. Proof of Address (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Driving License / Utility Bill < 3 months).
  3. Client Master List (CML) of Demat Account issued by Depository Participant (DP).
  4. Original Cancelled Cheque leaf with pre-printed name OR Bank Passbook/Statement attested by Bank.
* **Turnaround Timeline:** 21 calendar days from date of receipt by RTA.

---

## 2. Form ISR-2: Confirmation of Signature of Securities Holder by Bank Manager
* **Purpose:** Validation of signature when signature is missing, unrecorded, or mismatched in RTA records.
* **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Form ISR-2 filled and signed by the security holder.
  2. Attestation by Bank Manager with Manager Name, Employee Code, Bank Seal, Branch Address, Contact Number, and Date.
  3. Original Cancelled Cheque leaf or Bank Account Statement verified by the same bank branch.

---

## 3. Form ISR-3: Declaration of Opting-out of Nomination
* **Purpose:** Explicit declaration by physical share single holder to opt out of registering a nominee.
* **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Form ISR-3 signed by the sole holder.
  2. Witness signature (Name, Address, Signature of one witness).
* **Note:** SEBI strongly recommends registering a nominee via Form SH-13 instead of opting out.

---

## 4. Form ISR-4: Request for Issue of Duplicate Certificate and Other Service Requests
* **Purpose:** Processing requests for:
  - Issue of Duplicate Share Certificate (Lost/Stolen/Mutilated)
  - Claim from Unclaimed Suspense Account
  - Renewal / Exchange of Share Certificate
  - Endorsement / Sub-division / Splitting of Certificate
  - Consolidation of Folios / Certificates
  - Transmission / Transposition
* **Mandatory Procedures for Duplicate Share Certificate:**
  1. Form ISR-4 filled completely.
  2. Copy of FIR / Police Complaint registered for loss of certificates (if market value > ₹5 Lakhs).
  3. Advertisement in local newspaper (if market value > ₹5 Lakhs).
  4. Indemnity Bond on non-judicial stamp paper (Form ISR-4 Annexure).
  5. Letter of Confirmation (LOC) issued by RTA within 30 days; valid for 120 days to convert into demat.

---

## 5. Form ISR-5: Simplified Procedure for Transmission of Securities (Death of Holder)
* **Purpose:** Legal transmission of physical/demat securities upon the death of sole or joint holders.
* **Simplified Documentation Thresholds (SEBI Fourth LODR Amendment):**
  - **Up to ₹5,00,000 (Physical) / ₹15,00,000 (Demat) per issuer company:** Simplified documentation route (No Probate or Succession Certificate required).
  - **Above ₹5,00,000 (Physical) / ₹15,00,000 (Demat):** Requires Succession Certificate / Letter of Administration / Probated Will.
* **Mandatory Documents Required (Nominee Registered):**
  1. Form ISR-5 duly filled and signed by nominee.
  2. Original / Notarized Death Certificate of deceased holder.
  3. Self-attested PAN and Aadhaar of Nominee.
  4. Client Master List (CML) of Nominee's Demat Account.
* **Mandatory Documents Required (No Nominee - Legal Heirs):**
  1. Form ISR-5 signed by all legal heirs.
  2. Notarized Indemnity Bond from all legal heirs.
  3. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from non-claiming legal heirs (or Deed of Relinquishment).
  4. Legal Heirship Certificate / Family Tree certified by Revenue Authority / Tahsildar (for threshold limits).

---

## 6. Form SH-13 / Form ISR-13: Nomination Form for Registering a Nominee
* **Purpose:** Registration of a nominee for physical share folios under Section 72 of Companies Act 2013 and SEBI LODR.
* **Requirements:**
  1. Duly filled Form SH-13 with Folio number, Company Name, and Nominee details (Name, DOB if minor, Address, Relationship, PAN).
  2. Specimen signature of Nominee.
  3. Signatures of all joint holders and one witness.

---

## 7. Form SH-14: Cancellation or Variation of Nomination
* **Purpose:** Changing or cancelling an existing nominee registered on a physical share folio.
* **Requirements:**
  1. Duly filled Form SH-14 with Folio number, details of old nominee, and details of new nominee.
  2. Signatures of all joint holders and one witness.

---

## 8. Form IEPF-5 Instruction Sheet: e-Form Filing for Unclaimed Shares & Dividends
* **Purpose:** Detailed step-by-step procedure for reclaiming shares or dividends transferred to the IEPF Authority after 7 consecutive years of non-claim.
* **Filing Requirements:**
  1. File online e-Form IEPF-5 on MCA Portal (mca.gov.in) with Claimant PAN, Bank Account, CIN, Folio/DP-Client ID, Amount, Share Quantity.
  2. Submit physical dossier to Company Nodal Officer within 15 days containing: Printed IEPF-5, SRN slip, Original Share Certificates, Cancelled Cheque, Rs 1 Revenue Stamp Advance Receipt, CML of Demat Account, Aadhaar and PAN.
  3. Verification Report submitted by Company Nodal Officer to IEPF Authority within 30 days.
